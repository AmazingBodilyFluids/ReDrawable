Step 1 of 3

How is your tablet lying?

Put the tablet where it will sit on your desk, the way round you'll use it. Look at where the power button and the cable are, and pick the picture that matches. power button USB-C cable

Step 2 of 3

More targets make it more precise. The targets appear on the tablet screen, turned the way your tablet sits.

No tablet screen? Open the printable target sheet on the tablet instead (it's in the tablet folder).
